Privacy

Warship Duo collects no data. The game runs on your iPhone and does not need a network connection.

What the app does not do

There is no account, no sign-in, and no in-app server. The app does not include advertising or analytics, and it does not collect, sell, or share personal information. Gameplay stays on the device.

This website

This site is static information. It does not use analytics cookies or account sign-in.
