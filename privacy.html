Privacy

Warship Duo collects no data.

Warship Duo is a two-player game that runs entirely on your iPhone. It does not require a network connection, and it does not include accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

The app does not collect, sell, or share personal information. Gameplay stays on the device. There are no in-app servers to contact, and the app does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

This website is a simple informational page. It does not use analytics cookies or account sign-in.

If you have a privacy question, write to support@warshipduo.com.
