A two-player naval guessing game

Private fleets. One device. No connection required.

Warship Duo is an original, fully offline game for two people at the same iPhone. Place a custom fleet on a 9×9 grid, then take turns calling shots — without accounts, ads, or a network.

9×9 theater

Two ways to play

Folded play

On a foldable iPhone, fold the device to about 90 degrees. Warship Duo uses the system’s two-pane arrangement so each player can keep their own fleet private on their own pane.

Pass and play

On any iPhone, including models without a fold, pass the device between turns. A full-screen privacy cover hides the boards until the next player is ready.

Honest and offline

The game is two-player only and stays on the device. It does not include online matchmaking, accounts, or analytics. The fleet and rules are original — a 9×9 grid and invented vessel names — so this is not a licensed edition of any other naval game.
