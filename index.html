Warship Duo

Two players. One iPhone. Each hides a fleet.

On an iPhone Duo, fold the phone to about 90 degrees so each of you keeps a private pane. On any iPhone, pass it between turns — a cover hides the boards until the next captain is ready.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPhone Duo and iPhone. Fully offline.

  1. Folded play

    On a foldable iPhone, fold it to about 90 degrees. Each player uses their own pane and keeps their own fleet private.

  2. Pass and play

    On any iPhone, pass the device between turns. A full-screen “Hand it over” cover hides the boards until the next player is ready.

  3. A 9×9 theater

    Place five vessels, orthogonal only, touching allowed: Skipjack (2), Reefshadow (3), Tidefang (3), Ironwake (4), and Stormcrown (5). One shot a turn — Clear, Strike, Vessel lost, or Fleet defeated.

  4. Stays on the phone

    Two human players, same device. No accounts, no network, no data collected.
